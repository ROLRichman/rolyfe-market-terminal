① PREPARE · 🟡

Build the watchlist. Check higher timeframe, trend, levels, liquidity and events. A low oscillator alone is not an entry.

② WATCH · 🔵

Price approaches a level. Wait for candle close and agreement between trend, momentum and volume.

③ SNIPER CANDIDATE · 🟢

Several checks align. Review chart, entry trigger, invalidation, target ladder and position size. Not “go now.”

WAIT means the defined threshold setup has not triggered. Direction can still be bullish, bearish or mixed. No flag is a prediction or order.
